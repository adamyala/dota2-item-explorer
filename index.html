How is gold efficiency calculated?

Each stat is priced at the cheapest basic item that gives only that stat, e.g. Gauntlets of Strength (140 gold for 3 Strength) prices Strength at about 47 gold per point. An item's stats are added up at those prices and compared to its cost: over 100% means the stats alone are worth more than you pay. Stats with no basic item to price them (cast range, lifesteal, …) and abilities aren't counted, so items whose value is mostly their abilities score low. Hover a percentage for the breakdown.
